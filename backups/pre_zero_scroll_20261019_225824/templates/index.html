🛡️ Measurement Integrity & Hardware Contract

Hardware Baseline

Active Physical & Relative Sensors

  • DHT22 (GPIO 4): Real calibrated temperature (°C) & relative humidity (%).
  • Bare LDR (GPIO 35): Relative brightness (raw ADC). Classified as Dark (<250), Dim (250–1699), Bright (≥1700). Not calibrated lux.
  • MQ-135 (GPIO 34): Relative gas-response voltage via 10k:20k divider. Compared against rolling baseline. Not ppm, eCO₂, or certified AQI.
  • LM393 Sound (AO 32, DO 26): Peak-to-peak swing & threshold. Not calibrated sound pressure / dBA.

Explicitly Unsupported Channels (From Review 1)

  • Atmospheric Pressure: BME280 dropped in favor of DHT22. Exposes null.
  • eCO₂ & TVOC: ENS160 not present on hardware node. Exposes null.
  • Certified AQI: Requires multi-gas calibrated reference apparatus. Exposes null.
  • Calibrated Lux: BH1750 not installed; bare LDR voltage divider in use.
